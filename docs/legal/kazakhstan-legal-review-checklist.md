# Kazakhstan legal review checklist

**LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED** — questions for qualified counsel; not legal advice.

- Operator identity and privacy notice (Закон о персональных данных и др.)
- Cross-border processing (hosting, Google/Apple when enabled)
- Consent and electronic acceptance for Terms/Privacy
- UGC moderation duty and notice-and-action procedures
- Business listings accuracy and owner claims
- Account deletion and data subject rights
- Retention for orders, payments, audit logs
- Government/law-enforcement request handling
- Advertising and consumer protection (KZ)
- Age policy if minors targeted
- IP/copyright takedown process
